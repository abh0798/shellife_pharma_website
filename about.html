About Shellife Pharma

A dedicated eye care company built on the belief that every person deserves clear, healthy vision.

Who We Are

Committed to Eye Health

Shellife Pharma is a New Delhi-based pharmaceutical company specializing in ophthalmic solutions. We were founded with a singular focus — to develop and deliver high-quality, affordable eye care products that make a real difference in patients' lives.

Our product portfolio spans the full spectrum of eye care needs: from dry eye management and infection treatment to post-operative care and nutritional support. Each product is carefully formulated using clinically validated ingredients, ensuring both safety and efficacy.

We work closely with ophthalmologists, optometrists, and healthcare professionals across India to understand real-world clinical needs and translate them into effective solutions. Our commitment to quality is reflected in every bottle we produce.
